## Midterm Exam: Proposal Mock Defense Guidelines

Undergraduate Research 1 — Computer Science Track

## 💡 Presentation Rules & Preparation Checklist

* Source Material: All presentation content must come directly from your evaluated and approved Chapters 1, 2, and 3.

* Slide Rules: Keep text summarized and bite-sized (6x6 rule: ~6 bullets per slide, ~6 words per bullet). No full paragraphs on slides.

* Software Artifact Role: Serves as a testbed to prove algorithm/theory efficiency AND must include the UI/UX design needed to satisfy usability metrics during user testing.

* Target Completion Deadline: All groups must present their final study conclusions by the 1st week of April 2027.

* Time Limit: 15–20 minutes per group presentation + Panel Question & Answer (Q&A).

## 1. Slide-by-Slide Presentation Outline

Required Length: Exactly 11 Slides (Derived from Chapters 1–3)

SLIDE 1

## Title & Group Details

* Project title (Algorithm / Theoretical Focus)

* Names of all group members

* Adviser name and date of defense

SLIDE 2

## Background & Problem Statement (Ch. 1)

* Real-world context & current system bottlenecks

* Clear statement of the computational problem

* Main research question being addressed

SLIDE 3

## Objectives & Scope (Ch. 1)

* General objective (Overall algorithm goal)

* Specific actionable objectives

* Project bounds, limits, and exclusions

SLIDE 4

## Literature & Baseline Algorithms (Ch. 2)

* 2–3 existing baseline methods/algorithms

* Comparison table of current solutions

* The specific gap your study fills

SLIDE 5

## Expected Scientific Contribution

* Theoretical value added to Computer Science

* Practical benefits to developers or end-users

* Summary of tangible research deliverables

SLIDE 6

## Software Artifact & UI/UX Design (Ch. 3)

* System architecture diagram (Testbed layout)

* Tech stack (Language, libraries, execution environment)

* UI/UX Wireframes/Screenshots for user testing

SLIDE 7

## Testing Method & Metrics (Ch. 3)

* Algorithm Metrics: Speed, memory usage, accuracy

* Usability Metrics: User testing setup & survey tool

* Test datasets and participant evaluation plans

SLIDE 8

## Development & Testing Schedule (Ch. 3)

* Gantt Chart (Coding, Testing, Documentation)

* Proof of completion by 1st week of April 2027

* Feasibility defense of remaining workload

SLIDE 9

## Immediate Next Steps

* Immediate action items following proposal approval

* Specific targets for the next 2 to 4 weeks

* Upcoming milestones before full data gathering

SLIDE 10

## Technical Risks & Backup Plans (Ch. 3)

* Potential coding bugs or hardware bottlenecks

* Risks during user usability testing

* Clear contingency plans for delays

SLIDE 11

## References

* Clean, formatted reference list of all cited research literature and baseline software projects.

## Rubric 1: Presentation Material (Group Grade)

50 Points Total
Evaluates the overall quality, completeness, summarization, and timeline feasibility of the submitted slides.

Criteria
Exemplary (100%)
Proficient (85%)
Developing (70%)
Needs Work (50%)

Content Completeness & Source Compliance 15 Points
15 pts All 11 required slides are present. Content strictly matches approved Chapters 1–3 without gaps.
12.75 pts 10 slides present. Content mostly reflects approved Chapters 1–3 with minor gaps.
10.5 pts Missing 2–3 required slides, or includes information inconsistent with approved paper.
7.5 pts Major sections missing. Slides are incomplete or unaligned with Chapters 1–3.

Slide Design & Summarization 15 Points
15 pts Text is concise and bite-sized (bulleted). Excellent layout, easy to read, no walls of text.
12.75 pts Mostly bulleted text. Clean layout with minor overcrowded text on 1–2 slides.
10.5 pts Multiple slides contain long paragraphs and full sentences. Difficult to scan quickly.
7.5 pts Entire paragraphs are copy-pasted directly from the manuscript. Poor visual design.

Software UI/UX & Testbed Representation 10 Points
10 pts Clearly presents system testbed layout AND includes wireframes/UI designs needed for usability testing.
8.5 pts Shows system testbed and UI mockups, but details on user usability metrics are vague.
7 pts Shows system layout/architecture but lacks UI/UX screen designs for testing.
5 pts Missing artifact diagrams, tech stack details, and UI/UX representation.

Timeline Realism & Feasibility 10 Points
10 pts Clear timeline ending by 1st week April 2027. Immediate next steps and backup plans are realistic.
8.5 pts Timeline targets 1st week April 2027. Next steps or backup plans present with minor missing details.
7 pts Schedule is unrealistically tight for April 2027. Next steps or backup plans are overly vague.
5 pts No timeline provided or schedule fails to account for completion by April 2027.

## Rubric 2: Group Oral Presentation & Defense (Individual Grade)

50 Points Per Student
Evaluates each student individually on their delivery, subject mastery, Q&A defense performance, and group coordination.

Criteria
Exemplary (100%)
Proficient (85%)
Developing (70%)
Needs Work (50%)

Mastery of Content & Clarity 15 Points
15 pts Explains assigned concepts clearly in simple, precise terms without reading slides verbatim.
12.75 pts Clear explanation with occasional reliance on slide text. Demonstrates good subject knowledge.
10.5 pts Reads directly from slides most of the time. Explanation of technical logic is unclear.
7.5 pts Unprepared. Unable to explain assigned slides or concepts accurately.

Defense & Q&A Response 15 Points
15 pts Answers panel questions directly, accurately, and confidently. Defends technical choices well.
12.75 pts Answers panel questions correctly with minor assistance or prompting from teammates.
10.5 pts Struggles to answer basic panel questions about their assigned section of the study.
7.5 pts Unable to answer panel questions or defend research decisions.

Delivery & Professionalism 10 Points
10 pts Audible, confident voice tone, excellent eye contact/camera presence, and professional demeanor.
8.5 pts Clear delivery and polite manner. Minor issues with voice volume or eye contact.
7 pts Hard to hear, speaks too fast/slow, or shows noticeable lack of engagement.
5 pts Unprofessional conduct or complete lack of vocal engagement.

Coordination & Time Management 10 Points
10 pts Transitions smoothly to/from teammates. Group stays strictly within the 15–20 min time limit.
8.5 pts Good transitions between members. Presentation is slightly over or under the time limit.
7 pts Awkward hand-offs between members. Significantly exceeds or falls short of time limit.
5 pts Disorganized hand-offs. Group disregards the assigned 15–20 minute time window.
