## 📝 Module Description

The final phase of your journey focuses on communication and defense. Writing a great proposal is only half the battle; you must also be able to advocate for your design choices. This module teaches you how to optimize technical visual aids, replace heavy text blocks with clean UML models/flowcharts, and confidently handle unscripted cross-examinations from a faculty panel.

You will practice distilling your 40-to-60 page manuscript into a sharp, 3-minute tech elevator pitch. Following your formal defense, you will learn the professional workflow of mapping panel feedback into a "Response-to-Reviewers" matrix to produce a finalized, camera-ready manuscript package ready for implementation in Research 2.

## 🎯 Module Intended Learning Outcomes (MILOs)

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

* 
Synthesize their proposal into a clear oral presentation and supporting visuals.

* 
Defend their research choices confidently in a question-and-answer format.

* 
Finalize the written proposal incorporating defense feedback and ensure readiness for official submission.

* 
Demonstrate effective communication tailored to both technical and semi-technical audiences.
