# Midterm Exam: Proposal Mock Defense Guidelines

Undergraduate Research 1 — Computer Science Track

### 💡 Presentation Rules & Preparation Checklist

- **Source Material:** All presentation content must come directly from your evaluated and approved **Chapters 1, 2, and 3**.
- **Slide Rules:** Keep text summarized and bite-sized (6x6 rule: ~6 bullets per slide, ~6 words per bullet). **No full paragraphs on slides.**
- **Software Artifact Role:** Serves as a testbed to prove algorithm/theory efficiency **AND** must include the UI/UX design needed to satisfy usability metrics during user testing.
- **Target Completion Deadline:** All groups must present their final study conclusions by the 1st week of April 2027.
- **Time Limit:** 15–20 minutes per group presentation + Panel Question & Answer (Q&A).

## Rubric 1: Presentation Material (Group Grade)

50 Points Total

Evaluates the overall quality, completeness, summarization, and timeline feasibility of the submitted slides.

| Criteria | Exemplary (100%) | Proficient (85%) | Developing (70%) | Needs Work (50%) |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| **Content Completeness & Source Compliance** 15 Points | **15 pts** All 11 required slides are present. Content strictly matches approved Chapters 1–3 without gaps. | **12.75 pts** 10 slides present. Content mostly reflects approved Chapters 1–3 with minor gaps. | **10.5 pts** Missing 2–3 required slides, or includes information inconsistent with approved paper. | **7.5 pts** Major sections missing. Slides are incomplete or unaligned with Chapters 1–3. |
| **Slide Design & Summarization** 15 Points | **15 pts** Text is concise and bite-sized (bulleted). Excellent layout, easy to read, no walls of text. | **12.75 pts** Mostly bulleted text. Clean layout with minor overcrowded text on 1–2 slides. | **10.5 pts** Multiple slides contain long paragraphs and full sentences. Difficult to scan quickly. | **7.5 pts** Entire paragraphs are copy-pasted directly from the manuscript. Poor visual design. |
| **Software UI/UX & Testbed Representation** 10 Points | **10 pts** Clearly presents system testbed layout AND includes wireframes/UI designs needed for usability testing. | **8.5 pts** Shows system testbed and UI mockups, but details on user usability metrics are vague. | **7 pts** Shows system layout/architecture but lacks UI/UX screen designs for testing. | **5 pts** Missing artifact diagrams, tech stack details, and UI/UX representation. |
| **Timeline Realism & Feasibility** 10 Points | **10 pts** Clear timeline ending by 1st week April 2027. Immediate next steps and backup plans are realistic. | **8.5 pts** Timeline targets 1st week April 2027. Next steps or backup plans present with minor missing details. | **7 pts** Schedule is unrealistically tight for April 2027. Next steps or backup plans are overly vague. | **5 pts** No timeline provided or schedule fails to account for completion by April 2027. |

## Rubric 2: Group Oral Presentation & Defense (Individual Grade)

50 Points Per Student

Evaluates each student individually on their delivery, subject mastery, Q&A defense performance, and group coordination.

| Criteria | Exemplary (100%) | Proficient (85%) | Developing (70%) | Needs Work (50%) |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| **Mastery of Content & Clarity** 15 Points | **15 pts** Explains assigned concepts clearly in simple, precise terms without reading slides verbatim. | **12.75 pts** Clear explanation with occasional reliance on slide text. Demonstrates good subject knowledge. | **10.5 pts** Reads directly from slides most of the time. Explanation of technical logic is unclear. | **7.5 pts** Unprepared. Unable to explain assigned slides or concepts accurately. |
| **Defense & Q&A Response** 15 Points | **15 pts** Answers panel questions directly, accurately, and confidently. Defends technical choices well. | **12.75 pts** Answers panel questions correctly with minor assistance or prompting from teammates. | **10.5 pts** Struggles to answer basic panel questions about their assigned section of the study. | **7.5 pts** Unable to answer panel questions or defend research decisions. |
| **Delivery & Professionalism** 10 Points | **10 pts** Audible, confident voice tone, excellent eye contact/camera presence, and professional demeanor. | **8.5 pts** Clear delivery and polite manner. Minor issues with voice volume or eye contact. | **7 pts** Hard to hear, speaks too fast/slow, or shows noticeable lack of engagement. | **5 pts** Unprofessional conduct or complete lack of vocal engagement. |
| **Coordination & Time Management** 10 Points | **10 pts** Transitions smoothly to/from teammates. Group stays strictly within the 15–20 min time limit. | **8.5 pts** Good transitions between members. Presentation is slightly over or under the time limit. | **7 pts** Awkward hand-offs between members. Significantly exceeds or falls short of time limit. | **5 pts** Disorganized hand-offs. Group disregards the assigned 15–20 minute time window. |
