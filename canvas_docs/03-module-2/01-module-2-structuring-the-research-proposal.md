## 📝 Module Description

Now that you have isolated a research gap, it is time to build your technical blueprint. This module deconstructs the macro-structure and anatomy of a standard computer science research proposal. You will learn the art of crafting precise technical abstracts, framing strong introductions, and shifting from basic summaries to a critical, unified literature synthesis matrix.

A major portion of your time will be spent on research design and methodology, where you will map out your project’s system architecture, operational pipelines, data collection frameworks, and explicit performance evaluation metrics. Finally, you will practice the stylistic conventions of high-level technical writing for computing.

## 🎯 Module Intended Learning Outcomes (MILOs)

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

* 
Identify and explain the standard components of a research proposal (title, abstract, introduction, literature review, methodology, etc.).

* 
Draft each major section of a proposal with appropriate depth and structure.

* 
Integrate reviewed literature to justify the research gap and support chosen approaches.

* 
Design a methodology appropriate to the research problem, including data sources, tools, and evaluation criteria.
