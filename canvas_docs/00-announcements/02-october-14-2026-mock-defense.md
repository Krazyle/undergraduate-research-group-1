## Mock Defense Reminders & Guidelines

Undergraduate Research 1

Dear Class,

As we prepare for your upcoming **Mock Defense**, please strictly observe the following guidelines during your presentation:

**1. Strict Time Limit (15–20 Minutes)**

Each group will be strictly limited to **15 to 20 minutes** for their presentation. Rehearse with your group beforehand to ensure you manage your time effectively.

**2. Presentation Outline**

Follow the prescribed presentation outline provided in class.

**3. Group Participation**

Every group member must be assigned a speaking role and present a portion of the research.

**4. Presentation Delivery & Cue Cards**

You may use cue cards or notes for reference, but you should **not spend the majority of your time reading** directly from them or from your slides. Practice maintaining good eye contact and presentation flow.

Use this mock defense opportunity to gain feedback and refine your work before your actual defense. Good luck with your preparations!
