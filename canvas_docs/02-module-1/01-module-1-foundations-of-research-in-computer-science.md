### 📝 Module Description

Welcome to your first step in scientific inquiry! This module introduces you to the core definition and purpose of research within the fields of computer science, engineering, and broader society. You will learn to navigate different research paradigms—specifically comparing Quantitative, Qualitative, Mixed Methods, Experimental, Applied, and Design Science approaches.

The primary focus of this module is transforming your broad interests into a tightly scoped, highly viable research question. You will learn strategies for identifying literature gaps, constructing advanced query strings inside digital repositories (like IEEE Xplore and ACM Digital Library), and mastering academic integrity through reference management software like Zotero.

### 🎯 Module Intended Learning Outcomes (MILOs)

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

- **Explain** the role and importance of research in computer science and its societal impact.

- **Differentiate** among common research types and methodologies (e.g., experimental, exploratory, applied, qualitative vs. quantitative) in computing contexts.

- **Formulate** a clear research problem or question grounded in a gap or need in existing computing knowledge or practice.

- **Conduct** preliminary literature searches to situate the problem and identify related work.
