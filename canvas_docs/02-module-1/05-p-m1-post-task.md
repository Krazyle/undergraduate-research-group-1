### 📌 Activity Background & Objectives

In Activity 1, your group established team roles and pitched preliminary computing problems. In our lecture (*Module 1: Foundations of CS Research*), we learned that computer science research is not merely building a software product (Software Engineering)—it requires a **novel component/experiment**, an **empirical evaluation**, and a clear contribution to knowledge.

By completing this activity, your group will:

- Refine your chosen preliminary title into a scholarly title emphasizing research/experimental evaluation rather than commercial app development.
- Convert your problem statement into a **Funnel-Scoped Main Research Question (RQ)** and 1–2 measurable sub-questions.
- Locate and cite **one (1) peer-reviewed foundational literature source** using proper Boolean search techniques and APA 7th Edition formatting.

### 📝 Instructions & Group Tasks

#### Part 1: Title Refinement (8 Points)

- State your group's original title selected from Activity 1.
- Evaluate your title: Does it sound like a standard software development project, or does it highlight a novel research artifact, algorithm, or empirical evaluation?
- Write your **Revised Research Title** ensuring it clearly specifies the core technical contribution, variables/metrics, and application context.

#### Part 2: Scoped Research Questions (12 Points)

- Summarize the core **research gap** from your problem statement (i.e., why existing tools fail or what unresolved problem you are measuring/testing).
- Formulate a **Main Research Question (Main RQ)** using the "Funnel Approach" to narrow down your focus.
- Formulate **1–2 Specific Sub-Questions (Sub-RQs)** targeting actionable experimental evaluations (e.g., execution latency, route optimization accuracy, cost reduction, or signal attenuation modeling).

**💡 Tip for Strong RQs:** Ensure your RQs cannot be answered with a simple "Yes/No." Instead, use formats like *"How does Algorithm X compare to Baseline Y in terms of Metric Z?"* or *"To what extent does Model A reduce Latency B under condition C?"*

#### Part 3: Related Peer-Reviewed Literature (10 Points)

- Search IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Google Scholar, or Scopus to find **ONE (1) peer-reviewed paper** (published within the last 5–7 years) relevant to your main RQ.
- Construct and include the exact **Boolean Search String** (using `AND`, `OR`, and quotes `""`) you used to find the paper.
- Provide the complete **APA 7th Edition citation**.
- Write a 2–3 sentence **Key Findings Summary** and a 2–3 sentence **Relevance Justification** detailing how this paper grounds or benchmarks your study.
