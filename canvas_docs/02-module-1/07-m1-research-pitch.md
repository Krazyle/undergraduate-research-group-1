# Assignment: CS Research Pitch Video

Undergraduate Research 1  \|  **50 Total Points**  \|  **Duration: 15–25 Minutes**

**Submission Types:** Media Recording or Web Link (Canvas Studio, Zoom cloud link, YouTube Unlisted, Google Drive, or Panopto).

## 1. Overview & Objectives

In **Undergraduate Research 1**, communicating your technical vision is as vital as developing your computational methodology. In this activity, you will record a **15 to 25-minute comprehensive Research Pitch Video** detailing your proposed CS research project.

Your goal is to convince a technical audience (faculty, peer researchers, or software engineers) that your research problem is significant, your proposed CS methodology is sound, your evaluation plan is quantitative, and your project scope is feasible for the undergraduate timeline.

**⚠ MANDATORY PRESENTATION REQUIREMENT:**

Your video MUST feature a screen recording of your slides/diagrams **AND your webcam video feed (presenter's face / talking head overlay) visible throughout the entire presentation**. Audio-only presentations or slides recorded without the presenter's face on screen will receive an automatic **10-point penalty**.

## 2. Required Video Content & Time Structure

Structure your 15–25 minute recording into the following five core sections:

- **1. Title & Problem Motivation (3–4 mins):** Introduce yourself and clearly frame the real-world or theoretical computational bottleneck driving your work.
- **2. Background & Research Gap (3–4 mins):** Review current state-of-the-art CS solutions and explain where current algorithms, systems, or frameworks fall short.
- **3. Proposed CS Methodology & Architecture (5–7 mins):** Provide a deep dive into your technical approach. *Mandatory: You must walk through a System Architecture Diagram / Pipeline Flowchart and present a concrete toy example or pseudocode snippet.*
- **4. Experimental Evaluation & Metrics Plan (3–4 mins):** Define your baseline algorithms, software/hardware testbeds, and exact quantitative evaluation metrics (e.g., runtime complexity, latency, accuracy, throughput, memory overhead).
- **5. Feasibility, Timeline & Expected Contributions (2–3 mins):** Outline your semester-by-semester roadmap (UR1 & UR2 milestones), risk mitigations, and expected contributions.

## 3. Technical & Recording Guidelines

- **Duration:** 15:00 to 25:00 minutes. (Videos under 15 minutes or over 25 minutes will incur duration penalties).
- **Recommended Recording Tools:** Canvas Studio, Zoom (Record to Computer with Speaker + Shared Screen), OBS Studio, Loom, or Panopto.
- **Visuals:** Slides must be readable with high-resolution system diagrams. Position your webcam bubble so it does not cover slide text.
- **Target Audience:** Pitch to a *Senior CS Undergrad / Junior Developer* technical level. Use precise CS terminology without unneeded jargon.
