## 01. The Role and Impact of CS Research

Building vs. Discovering: Research is a systematic process of investigation used to discover new facts, solve problems, test theories, or establish new knowledge[cite: 1]. At its core, research isn't just about collecting existing information—it is about asking a focused question and gathering evidence to answer it in a structured, repeatable way[cite: 1].

🔄 The Iterative Research Cycle

Observation ➔ Question ➔ Hypothesis ➔ Experiment ➔ Analysis ➔ Conclusion[cite: 1]

## Why Research Matters

⚡ Drives innovation and new capabilities[cite: 1]
🛡️ Strengthens cybersecurity and resilience[cite: 1]
⚙️ Optimizes infrastructure and performance[cite: 1]

## 03. From Interest to Research Question

## What Makes a Strong Research Question?

## 1. Relevant & Original

Addresses a genuine gap in literature, solves a practical problem, or offers a fresh perspective[cite: 1].

❌ Overdone: "What factors cause stress in college students?"[cite: 1]

🟢 Novel: "How do remote asynchronous testing environments impact test anxiety levels among first-year CS students?"[cite: 1]

## 2. Clear & Specific

Leaves no ambiguity about what, who, or where is being investigated[cite: 1].

❌ Vague: "How does technology affect learning?"[cite: 1]

🟢 Specific: "How does daily interactive tablet usage impact reading comprehension scores among 3rd-grade students?"[cite: 1]

## 3. Focused & Researchable

Targets defined relationships between variables that can be observed/measured. Not purely philosophical[cite: 1].

❌ Unresearchable: "Should AI be banned in schools?"[cite: 1]

🟢 Researchable: "What effect does AI-assisted writing software have on the grammatical accuracy of ESL undergraduate students?"[cite: 1]

## 4. Feasible

Can realistically be answered within your constraints (time, data access, budget, tech)[cite: 1].

❌ Unfeasible: "How will quantum computing impact global financial markets over the next 50 years?"[cite: 1]

🟢 Feasible: "How does Algorithm X compare to Algorithm Y in runtime execution when processing a 100,000-record dataset?"[cite: 1]

## 5. Complex & Analytical

Requires analysis, synthesis, and interpretation rather than a simple lookup of facts[cite: 1].

❌ Simple Lookup: "Is Python faster than C++ for web development?"[cite: 1]

🟢 Complex: "How does memory overhead affect system throughput when deploying microservice architectures versus monolithic architectures in low-resource environments?"[cite: 1]
